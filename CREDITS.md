## Sound

`audio/soundtrack.m4a` is the final mix from the published video. The music is synthesised in code
(`audio/synth.py`, `audio/lib_music.py`, `audio/make_music.py`); the effects are built by
`audio/make_sfx_*.py` from about a hundred short samples taken from the free packs below, plus
public-domain / CC field recordings from archive.org (radio aporee ::: maps) for rain, thunder, wind
and surf.

The per-file catalogue that mapped every sample to its pack was a build artefact and did not survive,
so the packs are credited collectively. Most are CC0; a minority are CC-BY 3.0/4.0 or CC-BY-SA 3.0/4.0
— the licence is on each pack's page. Because of the share-alike samples, **the soundtrack file is
distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)**, not MIT.

**Kenney** (CC0) — https://kenney.nl/assets : Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, UI Audio, Digital Audio,
Sci-Fi Sounds, RPG Audio, Music Jingles, Casino Audio.

**Tone.js / audio** — drum samples, https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/drum-samples
